- Post carousels/videos: 10 AM - 12 PM ET or 6 PM - 8 PM ET

- Use all 30 hashtags (mix of high, medium, low competition)
- Always include link in bio + "Link in bio" in caption
- Reply to every comment within first hour for algorithm boost

### LinkedIn:
- Post: 7 AM - 9 AM ET or 12 PM - 1 PM ET (lunch time)
- Use 3-5 hashtags max (less is more on LinkedIn)
- Tag relevant companies/people when appropriate
- Share to LinkedIn Stories for extra reach
- Engage with commenters - LinkedIn rewards conversation

### Video Best Practices:
- First 3 seconds = hook (must stop the scroll)
- Captions ON (80% watch without sound)
- Square (1:1) or vertical (9:16) format
- Keep under 60 seconds for feed, 15-30 for stories
- End with clear CTA

2. **Use Scheduling Tools:** Plan posts in Later, Planoly, or Meta Business Suite to stay consistent.

3. **Repurpose Everything:** One carousel can become:

   - A LinkedIn post
   - 5-10 individual Instagram stories
   - A blog post
