## WORKFLOW: FROM IDEA TO PUBLISHED

### PHASE 1: PLANNING (Weekly Meeting)
**Who:** Charmaine + Content Creator
**When:** Every Monday, 10:00 AM (60 min)
**Where:** Google Meet + Weekly Planning Template (Google Sheets)

**Steps:**
1. Review last week's analytics (Google Sheets dashboard)
2. Approve content in "READY FOR REVIEW" folder
3. Plan next week's content using Weekly Planning Template
4. Schedule shooting time (if needed)
5. Document action items

**Output:**
- ✅ Approved content moved to "APPROVED" folder
- ✅ Next week's content calendar filled out
- ✅ Shooting schedule confirmed
- ✅ Action items assigned

---

### PHASE 2: CONTENT CREATION (Mon-Wed)
**Who:** Content Creator
**When:** Monday-Wednesday
**Where:** Google Drive "IN PROGRESS" folder

**Steps:**
1. Create folder: "Week of [Date]"
2. Write captions using Caption Draft Template (Google Doc)
3. Design graphics (save as PNGs or PDFs)
4. Edit videos (export and upload)
5. Organize all files in week's folder

**Output:**
- ✅ Captions drafted (Google Doc)
- ✅ Graphics designed (PNGs/PDFs)
- ✅ Videos edited (MP4s)
- ✅ All files organized in week's folder

---

### PHASE 3: REVIEW & APPROVAL (Wed-Thu)
**Who:** Charmaine Powell
**When:** Wednesday-Thursday
**Where:** Google Drive "READY FOR REVIEW" folder

**Steps:**
1. Content Creator moves completed content to "READY FOR REVIEW"
2. Content Creator fills out Review Checklist (Google Sheets)
3. Charmaine reviews content (captions, designs, videos)
4. Charmaine leaves feedback directly in Google Docs (comments/suggestions)
5. Content Creator implements edits
6. Charmaine gives final approval (checkmark in Review Checklist)

**Output:**
- ✅ Feedback provided (Google Doc comments)
- ✅ Edits implemented
- ✅ Final approval given (Review Checklist)
- ✅ Content moved to "APPROVED - READY TO SCHEDULE" folder

---

### PHASE 4: SCHEDULING IN BUFFER (Thu-Fri)
**Who:** Content Creator
**When:** Thursday-Friday
**Where:** Buffer + Google Sheets (Content Calendar)

**Steps:**
1. Open "APPROVED - READY TO SCHEDULE" folder
2. Upload content to Buffer following upload instructions
3. Schedule posts according to Master Content Calendar
4. Copy Buffer post link
5. Paste Buffer link into Master Content Calendar (Google Sheets)
6. Move content to "PUBLISHED" folder (organized by month)
7. Update Content Calendar status to "Scheduled"

**Output:**
- ✅ Content scheduled in Buffer
- ✅ Buffer links recorded in calendar
- ✅ Content archived in "PUBLISHED" folder
- ✅ Calendar updated to "Scheduled" status

---

### PHASE 5: PUBLISHING & ENGAGEMENT (Ongoing)
**Who:** Content Creator (monitoring) + Charmaine (optional engagement)
**When:** Daily, throughout the week
**Where:** Instagram, LinkedIn, ManyChat

**Steps:**
1. Monitor posts as they publish (Buffer auto-posts)
2. Respond to comments within 2 hours
3. Trigger ManyChat automations for keyword comments
4. Pin top comment to encourage engagement
5. Share posts to Stories with engagement stickers
6. Track initial performance in Google Sheets

**Output:**
- ✅ Comments responded to
- ✅ ManyChat triggers activated
- ✅ Stories posted
- ✅ Initial metrics recorded

---

### PHASE 6: ANALYTICS & REPORTING (Weekly)
**Who:** Content Creator
**When:** Every Sunday (prep for Monday meeting)
**Where:** Google Sheets (Weekly Performance Dashboard)

**Steps:**
1. Export Instagram Insights (weekly)
2. Export LinkedIn Analytics (weekly)
3. Update Weekly Performance Dashboard (Google Sheets)
4. Identify top-performing content
5. Note insights and action items for next week
6. Share dashboard with Charmaine before Monday meeting

**Output:**
- ✅ Dashboard updated with week's data
- ✅ Top performers identified
- ✅ Insights & action items documented
- ✅ Ready for weekly meeting discussion

TROUBLESHOOTING

Solution: Check file format (MP4), re-export if needed
