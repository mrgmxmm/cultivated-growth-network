## Design Specifications

### Visual Style:
- **Font:** Inter Bold for titles, Inter Regular for descriptions
- **Colors:**
  - Hook titles: White text on CGN Green (#099437) or CGN Yellow (#ECA310)
  - Descriptions: Charcoal (#333333) on white or soft nature backgrounds
- **Layout:**
  - Title: Top half, large and bold with subtle nature overlay (leaves, seeds, roots)
  - Description: Bottom half, clean and readable
  - CTA: Bottom corner with subtle arrow
- **Branding:** CGN logo watermark in corner
- **Size:** 1080x1920px (vertical video format for Reels/TikTok)

### Animation (for video version):
- Nature background video (seeds sprouting, leaves growing, water flowing)
- Title appears with fade + slight zoom (0-2 seconds)
- Description types on or fades in line by line (2-6 seconds)
- CTA slides in from bottom (6-8 seconds)
- Hold final frame for 2 seconds
- Total video length: 10-12 seconds

## Content Strategy Notes

### "Low Hanging Fruit" Approach:
These postcards are designed to:
✓ Deliver value without revealing full methodology
✓ Create curiosity about the frameworks (SEEDS, ROOTS, BRANCH)
✓ Position Charmaine as a trusted guide
✓ Make people WANT to learn more
✓ Feel saveable and shareable (inspirational + tactical hint)

### What They DON'T Reveal:
- Specific steps within SEEDS Method™
- Exact ROOTS coaching process
- Detailed BRANCH implementation phases
- Complete scripts or templates
- Full diagnostic tools

### What They DO Provide:
- Philosophy shifts (force → cultivate)
- High-level principles (consistency, trust, integration)
- Permission to try a different approach
- Validation of pain points
- Clear invitation to go deeper

## Success Metrics

### What to Track:
- **Saves:** Target 60+ per post (saveable wisdom)
- **Shares:** Target 25+ per post (inspirational + relatable)
- **Comments:** Target 40+ per post (emotional resonance)
- **Profile Visits:** Target 100+ per post (curiosity activation)
- **DM Triggers:** Target 20+ per post (high intent)

### Optimization:
- If saves are low: Add more tactical hints in the two-sentence add-on
- If comments are low: Make titles more provocative/contrarian
- If shares are low: Strengthen emotional connection in visual style
- If DMs are low: Make CTAs more specific and valuable
