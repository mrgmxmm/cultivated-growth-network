## Design Specifications (All Carousels)

### Typography
- **Headlines:** Inter Bold, 32-40px
- **Body Text:** Inter Regular, 16-18px
- **Accents/Labels:** Inter SemiBold, 14-16px
- **Line Height:** 1.5-1.6 for readability

### Color Usage
- **Primary Backgrounds:** White (#FFFFFF), Light Grey (#F5F5F5)
- **Accent Colors:** CGN Yellow (#ECA310), CGN Green (#099437)
- **Text:** Charcoal (#333333) for body, Black (#000000) for headlines
- **Borders/Accents:** Light Yellow (#F1D396), Light Green (#C6FFD9)

### Spacing & Layout
- **Margins:** 60px minimum on all sides
- **Text Alignment:** Left-aligned for readability (center only for cover pages)
- **Icon Size:** 48-64px for supporting graphics
- **Logo Watermark:** 40px height, 20% opacity, bottom right corner

### Visual Elements
- **Icons:** Line-based, simple, brand colors
- **Gradients:** Smooth, professional (Yellow → Green for growth themes)
- **Borders:** 4-6px accent bars when used
- **Textures:** Subtle, never overpowering the text
