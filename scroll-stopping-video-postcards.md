## Design Specifications

### Visual Style:
- **Font:** Inter Bold for titles, Inter Regular for descriptions
- **Colors:**
  - Hook titles: White text on CGN Green (#099437) or CGN Yellow (#ECA310)
  - Descriptions: Charcoal (#333333) on white or light backgrounds
- **Layout:**
  - Title: Top third, large and bold
  - Description: Middle section, readable size
  - CTA: Bottom third with arrow or button graphic
- **Branding:** CGN logo watermark in corner
- **Size:** 1080x1080px (Instagram) or 1080x1350px (Instagram portrait)

### Animation (for video version):
- Title appears first with zoom/slide effect (0-2 seconds)
- Description fades in line by line (2-5 seconds)
- CTA appears with attention-grabbing motion (5-7 seconds)
- Hold final frame for 2-3 seconds

## Success Metrics

### What to Track:
- **Saves:** Target 50+ (indicates high value)
- **Shares:** Target 20+ (amplification)
- **Comments:** Target 100+ (engagement + lead gen)
- **Profile Visits:** Target 200+ (awareness)
- **DM Triggers:** Target 30+ (conversion intent)

### Optimization:
- If saves are low: Content needs more actionable value, add practical tips
- If comments are low: Not resonating emotionally, strengthen the connection to their growth journey
- If shares are low: Not inspiring enough, make titles more uplifting and shareable
- If DMs are low: CTA isn't clear enough, make the invitation more welcoming
