## Posting Strategy

### When to Post These

**OPTION 1: Launch Sequence**
- **Day 1:** Carousel 2 (What this channel is about) - Sets expectations first
- **Day 2:** Carousel 1 (CGN introduction) - Then introduces the brand
- **Day 3:** Begin Week 1 introduction plan (Charmaine's story)

**OPTION 2: Immediate Welcome**
- **Post 1:** Carousel 1 (CGN introduction) - Brand first
- **Post 2:** Carousel 2 (Channel purpose) - Then expectations
- **Post 3:** Charmaine introduction from Week 1 plan

**RECOMMENDED:** Option 1
*Why:* People need to know what they're following BEFORE they decide to follow. Set expectations first, then introduce the brand depth.

---

### Best Times to Post
- **LinkedIn:** Tuesday or Thursday, 9-11 AM EST
- **Instagram:** Monday or Wednesday, 10 AM - 12 PM EST

### Engagement Strategy
1. **Pin your intro comment** asking "What brought you here today?"
2. **Respond to every comment** within first 2 hours
3. **Use Stories** to drive traffic to posts:
   - Story 1: "New here? Start with this post 👉"
   - Story 2: Repost slide 2 or 3 with engagement sticker
   - Story 3: "What do you want to learn about sales?" question box
4. **Follow up in DMs** with anyone who comments with a specific question

## Design Specifications

### Typography
- **Headers:** Inter Bold, 28-36px
- **Body Text:** Inter Regular, 16-18px
- **Small Text/Captions:** Inter Medium, 14px
- **Line Height:** 1.5-1.7

### Color Palette
- **Slide 1:** White/Light gradient background
- **Slide 2 (Carousel 1):** CGN Green (#099437) with white text
- **Slide 3 (Carousel 1):** CGN Yellow (#ECA310) with charcoal text
- **Slide 1 (Carousel 2):** White with yellow header bar
- **Slide 2 (Carousel 2):** CGN Green with white text

### Visual Elements
- **Logo:** Centered, large, breathing room
- **Icons:** Seedling (🌱), checkmarks (✓ in CGN Green)
- **Spacing:** Minimum 40px margins, generous line spacing
- **Alignment:** Mix of center (headers, CTAs) and left (lists, body)
