## Posting Strategy for Filler Posts

### Content Mix Recommendation
- **2 Carousels per week** (Tuesday, Thursday)
- **3-4 Single Posts per week** (Monday, Wednesday, Friday, Sunday)
- **1 Story/Reel per day** (repurposed from carousels or quick tips)

### Weekly Content Calendar Example
| Day | Content Type | Theme |
|-----|--------------|-------|
| Monday | Single Post | Motivation/Goal Setting |
| Tuesday | Carousel | Educational Framework |
| Wednesday | Single Post | Quick Tip or Engagement Question |
| Thursday | Carousel | Problem/Solution Deep-Dive |
| Friday | Single Post | Quote or Stat |
| Saturday | Repost/Reshare | Best-performing content from past |
| Sunday | Single Post | Personal/BTS or Resource Offer |

### Engagement Best Practices
1. **Post single-image content during "off-peak" times** to maintain visibility throughout the day
2. **Use engagement questions on Wednesdays** (mid-week engagement boost)
3. **Resource offers on Fridays** (weekend DM nurturing opportunity)
4. **Motivational content on Mondays** (start the week strong)

### Design Consistency Checklist
- [ ] Brand colors only (Yellow, Green, neutrals)
- [ ] Inter font family
- [ ] CGN logo watermark (bottom right, 20% opacity)
- [ ] Clean, uncluttered layout
- [ ] High contrast for readability
- [ ] 1080x1080px sizing for both platforms
