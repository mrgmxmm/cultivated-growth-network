## SLIDE 2: Win #1 - The Power of the Pause

**Header**: Client Win #1

**Quote**:
"I stopped talking after asking the question, and they literally told me their entire problem—and how to solve it."

**Takeaway**:
One of my clients was rushing through discovery calls, filling every silence with more questions or explanations. This week, they practiced the 5-second pause after asking a key question. The result? Prospects opened up, shared deeper pain points, and basically outlined the exact solution they needed to buy.

**Aligned with**:
SEEDS - **Story** (Listening creates the space for the prospect's story to unfold)

**What to Implement**:
Next week: After asking "What's the biggest challenge you're facing right now?"—count to 5 in your head before saying anything else. Just try it once.

---

## SLIDE 3: Win #2 - Naming the Problem

**Header**: Client Win #2

**Quote**:
"When I started saying 'revenue inconsistency' instead of 'sales struggles,' everything clicked for them."

**Takeaway**:
This client was getting polite nods but no conversions. We refined their language to name the *specific* problem their ideal client actually thinks about at 2am. Within three conversations, they had two signed contracts because prospects said "That's exactly what I'm dealing with."

**Aligned with**:
SEEDS - **Empathy** (Naming their exact problem shows you truly understand their world)

**What to Implement**:
Write down the 3 most common problems your clients come to you with. Now write them in THEIR words—the exact phrase they'd use when venting to a friend.

---

## SLIDE 4: Win #3 - The Follow-Up Framework

**Header**: Client Win #3

**Quote**:
"I thought following up made me look desperate. Now I realize it makes me look professional."

**Takeaway**:
This client had 12 warm leads sitting in their inbox with no follow-up. We created a simple 3-touch framework: valuable insight on day 3, case study on day 7, direct ask on day 10. They closed 4 deals from those "dead" leads in two weeks—just by showing up consistently.

**Aligned with**:
ROOTS - **Consistency** (The fortune is in the follow-up, executed systematically)

**What to Implement**:
Pick ONE prospect you've been meaning to follow up with. Send them a 2-sentence email with a relevant article or insight—no ask, just value. Do it Monday.
