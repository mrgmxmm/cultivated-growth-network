THE PERMISSION CLOSE

Stop asking "Are you ready to move forward?"

TRY THIS INSTEAD:

RESULT FOR MY CLIENT:

Why? Because she was addressing concerns
in discovery, not waiting for objections at close.

Closing isn't convincing.
It's confirming what you've already built together.
