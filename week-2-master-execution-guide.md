## 🔧 TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

### If Engagement is Lower Than Expected:
- [ ] Increase Stories to 8-10 per day
- [ ] Go LIVE on Wednesday to boost visibility
- [ ] Engage more aggressively with target accounts (30-40/day)
- [ ] Boost Wednesday carousel with $50-100 ad spend
- [ ] Send email to your list driving traffic to Wednesday post

### If ManyChat Triggers Aren't Converting:
- [ ] Check that trigger words are set up correctly
- [ ] Test flows yourself to ensure they work
- [ ] Respond to DMs personally within 1 hour
- [ ] Add voice messages for personalization
- [ ] Simplify the CTA (make it more obvious)

### If Discovery Calls Aren't Booking:
- [ ] Follow up personally via DM (don't rely only on automation)
- [ ] Offer specific times (vs. just calendar link)
- [ ] Add urgency: "Limited coaching spots for Q1"
- [ ] Share more social proof in follow-ups
- [ ] Lower friction: Offer 15-min call vs. 30-min

## 📞 DISCOVERY CALL SCRIPT (For Week 2 Leads)

### Opening (Build Rapport)
"Hi [Name]! Thanks for booking time with me. I saw you downloaded the [3 Lessons Guide / ROOTS overview] from my post this week—what stood out most to you?"

### Discovery (Uncover Their Gap)
"Walk me through where you are right now with your sales process... And where do you want to be in the next 90 days?"

"What's stopping you from getting there?"

### Bridge to SEEDS/ROOTS
"Based on what you're describing, it sounds like the [specific SEEDS stage or ROOTS pillar] is where you need the most support. Does that resonate?"

### Present Solution
"Here's how we'd approach this together: [Explain relevant framework]. The goal is to move you from [current state] to [desired state] in [timeline]."

### Close (Permission Close)
"Based on what we've discussed, this sounds like the right fit. Let's talk about next steps—what does your timeline look like to get started?"
